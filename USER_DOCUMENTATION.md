# TechCare Services — User Guide

## Register

1. Open the app and wait for the splash screen.
2. On the login screen, tap **Don't have an account? Register**.
3. Enter your full name, email, 10-digit phone number, password (at least 8 characters), and address.
4. Tap **Create account**.
5. Return to login and sign in with your new credentials.

## Login

1. From the splash screen, you are taken to login if not already signed in.
2. Enter your **email or phone** and **password**.
3. Tap **Login** to open the TechCare Dashboard.

## Add a Device

1. From the dashboard, tap **Saved Devices**.
2. Tap **Add Device**.
3. Choose device type (Smartphone, Laptop, etc.), enter brand and model.
4. Tap **Save**. The device appears in your saved list.

## Browse Services

1. From the dashboard, tap **Browse Services**.
2. Use the category dropdown to filter by device type (or view All).
3. Each card shows service name, description, and estimated price.
4. Tap **Request Service** to start a repair request for that service.

## Request a Repair

1. From Browse Services, tap **Request Service** (or open Submit Repair from a service card).
2. Select your saved device and service.
3. Describe the issue (at least 10 characters).
4. Choose **Pickup** or **Service center drop-off**.
5. Enter preferred date (`yyyy-MM-dd`) and time (`HH:mm`).
6. Tap **Submit Request**. A technician is assigned automatically.

## Track a Booking

1. From the dashboard, tap **My Bookings**.
2. View your bookings with status, technician, and estimated completion.
3. Tap a booking to see full details, repair progress timeline, and status history.
4. Use **Update Status** on the details screen to simulate progress through repair stages.

## FAQs and Tips

1. From the dashboard, tap **FAQs & Tips**.
2. Read frequently asked questions about bookings, pickup, warranty, and tracking.
3. Scroll to maintenance tips grouped by device type.

## Contact Support

1. From the dashboard, tap **Support**.
2. View phone, email, and office address.
3. Fill in your name and message (at least 10 characters).
4. Tap **Send Message** — a confirmation appears when the form is valid.

## Logout

From the dashboard, tap **Logout** to end your session and return to the login screen.
